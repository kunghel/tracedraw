# Frame Generator

The Frame Generator draws a decorative frame round your design: ruled
lines for a certificate, classic scrolls for an invitation, art deco
steps, beads, a Greek key, scallops or interlaced waves.

## Drawing a frame {#frame}

Open the [[docker.frame_generator]] panel with
[[menu.effects > menu.effects.frame_generator]].

1. Select the objects to frame. With nothing selected, the frame goes
   inside the page.
2. Choose the [[docker.frame_style]]; the preview shows it on a small page.
3. Set the options:
   - [[docker.frame_corner]]: square, round, notched inward or cut across
     (for ruled lines, classic scrolls, beads and scallops).
   - [[docker.frame_margin]]: the gap between the objects and the frame,
     or between the page edge and the frame.
   - [[docker.frame_width]]: how wide the decorated band is.
   - [[docker.frame_line]]: the width of the thin lines.
   - [[docker.frame_repeat]]: the length of one bead, key, scallop or wave;
     each side gets a whole number of them.
   - [[docker.frame_side_ornaments]]: a diamond with two dots in the middle
     of each side.
   - [[docker.frame_color]]: the colour of the lines and ornaments.
4. Click [[docker.apply]].

The frame comes as one group of curves on top, selected, and one undo
removes it. Ungroup it to recolour or move its parts.
