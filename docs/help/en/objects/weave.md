# Weaving objects

Weave makes objects that cross pass over and under one another in turn, as
the rings of a chain or the strands of a knot do.

## Weaving {#weave}

Select two or more objects on one layer that cross (rings, ribbons, letters)
and choose [[menu.object > menu.object.order > menu.object.weave]]. Where two
of them overlap, the overlaps take turns: in every second one, counting round
the lower object, the lower object shows on top. The objects and the pieces
that show them on top become one group, in one step to undo.

Each piece is a copy of the lower object cut to its overlap, laid right above
the upper object. Fills and outlines both count: two rings drawn with thick
outlines link like a chain.

## Changing the weave {#change}

To change which object is on top at a crossing, undo, change the stacking
order of the objects (see [[menu.object.order]]), and weave again. To change
the objects themselves, ungroup, delete the pieces (named
"[[weave.piece_name]]"), edit, and weave again.
